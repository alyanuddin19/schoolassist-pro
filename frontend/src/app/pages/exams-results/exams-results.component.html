Pick the exam term and how many different classes may share a room and the system allocates the rest itself, in roll-number order: no class is ever parked whole in one room while another class is still waiting, every class is spread across the rooms as evenly as possible, each room mixes two or more classes whenever it can, and no room goes past its capacity. When a class's seats in a room are filled, it continues in the next room. Nothing is saved until you press Generate.

Priority rule: First maximize class mixing, then maximize room utilization.
